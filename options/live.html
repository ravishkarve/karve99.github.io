  1. Create an app at developers.kite.trade and set its redirect URL to
    http://127.0.0.1:8765/api/kite/callback
  2. Paste the app's API key and secret here and press Log in with Kite.
  3. Zerodha's own login page opens in a new tab. Sign in there with your user ID, password and TOTP. This page never sees your Zerodha password.

The free Personal plan shows your funds and positions. The live option chain and history need the paid Connect plan (₹500/month). Kite sessions end every morning, so you log in once a day. Read-only: no orders are ever placed.

The live option chain comes from Sensibull's public data and works without logging in. Logging in attaches your Sensibull session to every request the bridge makes. Sensibull signs in with an OTP or your broker, so the bridge opens a Sensibull window on your computer for you to finish the login.

Advanced: sign in with a session token instead
