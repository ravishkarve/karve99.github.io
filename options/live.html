Data source

Browsers cannot call Sensibull directly, so this page talks to a small bridge program running on your own computer. Start it once, then connect:

python3 options/bridge/sensibull_bridge.py

Then open 127.0.0.1:8765/options/live.html, or stay here if your browser allows this site to reach localhost. The live option chain works without logging in. Logging in attaches your Sensibull session to every request the bridge makes.

Advanced: sign in with a session token instead
